How to read this table
uplift is treatment − placebo, not minus baseline. headroom is the share of the remaining room that was filled; the placebo column is what length alone is worth.
activation is the share of runs, across the placebo and treatment arms, where the skill actually reached the context. Low activation drags uplift toward zero, because a treatment run that never loads the skill is a baseline run.
Auto activation and forced injection are two different experiments; the same package can have one row each. Those rows cannot be compared with each other, and shipping only the flattering one is how the ecosystem ended up with numbers nobody can reproduce.
Grade A (executable checks) and grade B (a pinned judge) are shown in separate groups, never in one ranking.
⚠️ means the placebo effect is larger than the net gain — most of that number is length.
